Ballot odds

What are your chances?

A queue number isn't a flat, and the ballot isn't a queue — it's a random draw. This tool takes the live application rate straight from HDB and tells you what it actually means for you.

Step one

Get the live rate from HDB

We don't store application rates here — they change every launch, so anything we saved would be out of date within months. Instead, take the real figure from the source.

HDB Flat Portal

During an application window, HDB publishes live application rates for every project and flat type, updated several times a day.

Open the Flat Portal ↗

Finding the right number

  1. Open the sales launch that's currently taking applications, then the project you're interested in.
  2. Find the application rate table. HDB breaks it down by flat type and by applicant type.
  3. Read your own row — not the headline. The project's overall figure mixes everyone together. You want the rate for your applicant type and your flat type. These differ enormously: in one June 2026 project, first-timers faced 3.3× while second-timers faced 23.9× for the same flats.
  4. Check near the end of the window. Rates climb as the deadline approaches, so a figure read on day one understates the competition. You can change your selection until applications close.
No launch open right now? Application rates only exist during an application window. Use the example figures further down to see how this works in the meantime.

Be careful with this

What this tool can't tell you

It's not a prediction

The ballot is a random draw. A good rate doesn't guarantee a flat and a bad one doesn't rule it out. This shows the shape of the competition, not your outcome.

Priority schemes aren't counted

HDB reserves slices of each project for specific groups — families with children, buyers living near parents. If you qualify, your real odds are better than the raw rate suggests.

A queue number isn't a flat

Succeeding in the ballot only earns you a turn to choose. If your number is high, the units you wanted may be gone before your turn comes.

Disclaimer

This simulator is an educational tool, not financial, legal or housing advice, and it is not affiliated with or endorsed by HDB. It takes a single published figure — the application rate — and explains what that figure means. It cannot account for priority scheme quotas, the exact split of supply between applicant groups, or how HDB's computerised ballot allocates within each pool.

The output is deliberately given as a rough band rather than a precise percentage, because the application rate alone cannot support a precise figure. Treat it as a sense of scale. For your actual application, rely on the live figures and guidance on the HDB Flat Portal, and on your HFE letter.
