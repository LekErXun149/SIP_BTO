Where these numbers come from

Two interest rates, two jobs. You pay 2.6% on an HDB loan, but HDB works out how much you may borrow using a 3.0% floor introduced in September 2022 to encourage prudent borrowing. The MSR and TDSR checks here use the stress figure, as HDB does. Bank borrowers are assessed against a stricter 4.0% floor set by MAS.

Same LTV, different cash. HDB and bank loans both cap at 75%, so the downpayment is 25% either way. The difference is what it can be paid with: an HDB loan's downpayment can come entirely from CPF OA, while a bank loan needs at least 5% of the price in hard cash however much CPF you hold.

The grant figure is — HDB publishes the maximum and the income ceiling but not the full band table, so it tapers evenly between them. Your actual grant is confirmed in your HFE letter.
