Pick up where you left off

Your progress is kept

Working out a BTO plan isn't a ten-minute job. Nothing you do here needs to be redone — and none of it is sent to us or anyone else.

AUTOMATIC

Saved as you go

Your calculator settings, journey stage and checklist ticks are stored in your own browser the moment you change them. Close the tab, come back tomorrow on the same device, and it's all still there.

PORTABLE

Save to a file

Press Save to file in the bar at the top and you'll get a small progress file in your downloads. Keep it as a backup, or move it to another device or browser.

ANYWHERE

Load it back

On any device, press Load file and pick that file. Everything is restored exactly as you left it — no account, no sign-up, no password.

Two things worth knowing

Automatic saving is per-device. Ticks made on your phone won't appear on your laptop, and clearing your browser data erases them. That's exactly what the progress file is for — it's the only way to carry your work across devices.

Your browser may warn you when closing the tab. That only happens when you have progress you haven't downloaded as a file yet. The wording comes from your browser, not from us, so it looks generic — it simply means "you might want to press Save to file first." Ignoring it is fine if you're staying on this device.
