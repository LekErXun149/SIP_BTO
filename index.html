About these numbers. KeyQuest is an educational tool built to make the BTO process easier to understand — it is not financial, legal, or housing advice. Figures reflect publicly available rules as of mid-2026: family income ceiling $14,000 (singles $7,000 for 2-room Flexi), EHG up to $80,000, HDB loan rate 2.6%, max 75% loan-to-value, and a 30% Mortgage Servicing Ratio cap. Grant and repayment figures are estimates only. Policies change, and your actual eligibility, grants, and loan depend on your HDB Flat Eligibility (HFE) letter. Always verify with HDB at hdb.gov.sg and the HDB Flat Portal before making any decisions.
