Before you switch routes

Eligibility, grants and loan limits differ between these routes. A resale flat has no income ceiling to purchase, but income still governs your grants and your HDB loan. Grant amounts also differ — the EHG applies to both new and resale flats, while the CPF Housing Grant for Resale Flats and the Proximity Housing Grant apply to resale only.

Check your own position before committing. Your HFE letter sets out what you qualify for on each route, and it is the only figure that counts.
